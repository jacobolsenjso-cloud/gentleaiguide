You cancel a ChatGPT subscription in the same place you started it. If you paid on the ChatGPT website, you cancel in ChatGPT's own settings. If you paid inside the app on an iPhone or iPad, you cancel through Apple. If you paid inside the app on an Android phone, you cancel through Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel anything — the payments carry on until you switch them off properly.

That's the whole answer. The rest of this page walks you through each route slowly, and explains what happens once you've cancelled.

## First, work out where you signed up

This is the step most people skip, and it's why cancelling sometimes seems not to work. ChatGPT can't cancel a subscription that Apple or Google is collecting, and the other way round.

If you don't remember, look at your bank or card statement for the monthly charge:

- If the charge mentions **OpenAI** or **ChatGPT**, you signed up on the website. Use the first set of steps below.
- If the charge mentions **Apple**, you signed up on an iPhone or iPad. Use the Apple steps.
- If the charge mentions **Google**, you signed up on an Android phone or tablet. Use the Google Play steps.

Still not sure? Start with the ChatGPT settings. If you subscribed through an app store, ChatGPT will usually tell you so and point you there.

One more case: if your employer or someone else pays for your account, it may be a work or team plan. That isn't yours to cancel — ask the person who set it up.

## Cancelling on the ChatGPT website

You can do this on a computer, or in a web browser on your phone or tablet. The exact words on the buttons change now and then, so look for the closest match rather than these exact labels.

1. Go to **chatgpt.com** and sign in with the same email address you used to pay.
2. Click your name or your small round profile picture. At the time of writing, it sits in a corner of the screen.
3. Choose **Settings**.
4. Look for the section about your **account** or your **plan**. You should see the name of the plan you pay for.
5. Click **Manage** (or something similar) next to it, then choose **Cancel subscription**.
6. Follow the steps to confirm. You may be asked why you're leaving — you don't have to give a reason.

When you're done, check your email. You should receive a confirmation that the subscription has been cancelled. Keep that email until the next billing date has passed without a charge.

## Cancelling on an iPhone or iPad

If you subscribed through the ChatGPT app on an Apple device, Apple handles the payment. You cancel in your device's settings, not in ChatGPT.

1. Open the **Settings** app (the grey gear wheel).
2. Tap **your name** at the very top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Find **ChatGPT** in the list and tap it.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**, then confirm.

If ChatGPT isn't in the list, you probably subscribed on the website or with a different Apple account. Go back and check your statement.

## Cancelling on an Android phone or tablet

If you subscribed through the ChatGPT app on an Android device, Google handles the payment.

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your **profile picture** in the top corner.
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Find **ChatGPT** and tap it.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the steps.

As with Apple, if ChatGPT isn't there, you most likely signed up somewhere else, or with a different Google account.

## What happens after you cancel

Cancelling stops the *next* payment. It doesn't usually end your paid features straight away. In most cases you keep them until the end of the period you've already paid for, and then your account quietly goes back to the free version.

That's worth knowing for two reasons.

**You don't lose your account.** Your free account stays, and so do your saved conversations. You can carry on using the free version of ChatGPT — which is good enough for most everyday jobs, as we explain in [do I need to pay?](/guides/do-i-need-to-pay/)

**Don't expect money back automatically.** Cancelling doesn't normally refund the month you're in. If you were charged by mistake, or charged after you cancelled, contact whoever took the payment: OpenAI's help pages if you paid on the website, or Apple or Google if you paid through their app store. Be polite and specific: say when you cancelled and when the charge appeared. If that gets nowhere, your bank can advise you on disputing a payment.

If you'd like to stop using ChatGPT altogether, cancel the subscription first, and only then think about deleting the account. Doing it in that order means you won't be left with a payment you can't find a way to stop. ## Watch out for fake "cancellation" help

Cancelling a subscription is exactly the sort of moment scammers like. Two things to keep in mind.

**Never call a "ChatGPT support" phone number you found by searching.** Some of the numbers that show up in search results are run by scammers who will ask you to read out card details or install software "to help". You don't need anyone on the phone to cancel. Every step above happens on your own screen, by you.

**Be wary of emails saying your subscription is about to renew.** If an email urges you to click a link to cancel or confirm payment, don't click it. Instead, go to chatgpt.com yourself, or open your phone's settings, and check there. If there's genuinely something to do, you'll see it. If you'd like a second pair of eyes, there's no shame in asking a family member or friend to sit with you while you do it. And if anything about AI still feels like a mystery, our guide to [whether AI is safe to use](/guides/is-it-safe/) is a good place to start.

## The short version

- Cancel where you signed up: ChatGPT's settings, Apple's settings, or the Google Play Store.
- Deleting the app does not stop the payments.
- You usually keep the paid features until the end of the month you've paid for, then drop back to the free version.
- Keep the confirmation email until the next billing date passes with no charge.
- Never phone a "support" number from a search, and never cancel through a link in an unexpected email.
